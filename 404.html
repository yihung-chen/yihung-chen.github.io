--- layout: page title: "Working Papers" permalink: /Working Papers/ --- ## Why Legislators Resign for Mayoral Races? A Study on Taiwan, 2008–2020 ![Taiwan electoral politics]({{ '/assets/img/level-hopping.jpg' | relative_url }}) *Political Parties · Electoral Politics*
Abstract Why do incumbent legislators seek executive office while retaining their legislative seats? This paper examines how party nomination rules shape legislators' decisions to pursue mayoral races in Taiwan. I argue that when electoral law permits legislators to retain their seats, party organizations play a central role in governing cross-level candidacies.
--- ## When the Signal Breaks: Origin-Country Elite Conflict and Vaccine Acceptance in Taiwan ![Vaccine politics]({{ '/assets/img/vaccine-politics.jpg' | relative_url }}) *Political Behavior · Public Opinion*
Abstract Why do people prefer vaccines developed in some countries over others, and can this preference be undermined by political conflict? This paper examines whether disputes among political elites in a vaccine's country of origin weaken the perceived credibility of its vaccines.
--- ## Backlash to Weaponized Non-Tariff Trade Barriers ![Trade barriers]({{ '/assets/img/trade-coercion.jpg' | relative_url }}) *Political Economy · Electoral Politics*
Abstract How does foreign economic coercion shape domestic political behavior? This paper examines the political consequences of China's restrictions on Taiwanese agricultural exports, focusing on whether economically targeted communities respond to trade pressure through changes in electoral support.
--- ## Foreign Economic Coercion and Domestic Coalition of Voluntary Regulatory Harmonization: The Case of Sugar Apple Export from Taiwan to China ![Sugar apple exports]({{ '/assets/img/sugar-apple.jpg' | relative_url }}) *Political Economy · Foreign Economic Coercion*
Abstract Why does foreign economic coercion sometimes induce domestic actors to voluntarily harmonize their regulatory practices with the demands of a coercing state? This paper examines Taiwan's sugar apple exports to China.
